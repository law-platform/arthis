資料1 件

書籍Law of application of the laws in general : Civil Code, book on the Law of Person司法省記録課

248.

Any person who has become the husband or wife by entering into the other house cannot again enter into a house other than the same even though his or her spouse is dead.

Such person can, however, return to his or her original house by obtaining the permission of the koshu of such house and that of the koshu of the house into which he or she has entered.
