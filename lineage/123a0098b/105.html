書籍Law of application of the laws in general : Civil Code, book on the Law of Person司法省記録課

105.

Any one whose quality of father and child has irrevocably been established before the time of the marriage of his or her father and mother has the rights of a legitimate child from the day of their marriage, and one whose quality has been so established after their marriage has the same rights from the day of such establishment.
