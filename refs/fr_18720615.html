Article 13. Agents de change must enter in their books the numbers of the securities which they buy or sell.
They must mention, upon the bordereaux d'achats, the numbers delivered. A reglement d'administration publique shall fix the rate of remuneration which shall be allowed to the Agent de change for such inscription of numbers.
