書籍Civil Code : book on the law of acquisition of property司法省

317.

An heir can in respect to a succession make a simple or limited acceptance or make a renunciation. A legal heir to katoku cannot, however, make a renunciation and an heir succeeding to katoku upon inkio cannot make a limited acceptance.
