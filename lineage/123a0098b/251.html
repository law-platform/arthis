書籍Law of application of the laws in general : Civil Code, book on the Law of Person司法省記録課

251.

Any person who has become a koshu by succession to katoku cannot abolish the house of which he is the koshu. Nevertheless, where the koshu of a main house is succeeded by the koshu of its branch house, or where there are any other good and sufficient reasons, a house can be abolished by obtaining the authorization of the Local Court.
