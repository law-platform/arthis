書籍Law of application of the laws in general : Civil Code, book on the Law of Person司法省記録課

282.

Where the absentee has appeared, or any tiding has been heard of such absentee, the effects of the declaration of absence cease at once.

Such absentee can recover his or her property in such state as it is then, and reclaim whatever its possessor has unduly enriched himself by disposing of it.
